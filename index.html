Privacy Policy for Morio

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Morio ("we", "us", or "our") provides reminder, productivity, AI, voice, and cloud features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Morio may collect or store and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Morio may collect or store:

  • Reminder titles, dates, times, and completion status
  • Voice recordings made for reminders
  • Chat messages and chat history
  • Custom personalities and preferences
  • Language, notification, timezone, and voice settings
  • Country information provided by you
  • Account and sign-in information
  • Premium access or subscription status
  • Basic device, app, and technical information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create, schedule, and deliver reminders
  • Store and sync your tasks and settings
  • Provide AI features and generate responses or reminder text
  • Provide text-to-speech and voice reminder features
  • Manage accounts and premium access
  • Show advertisements where applicable
  • Improve, secure, and maintain the app
  • Respond to support requests
  • Meet legal and security requirements

3. Voice Recordings and Microphone Access

Morio only uses the microphone when you choose to record a voice reminder.

We do not continuously listen to your microphone. Voice recordings may be stored on your device and, if you sign in and use cloud sync, may be uploaded to our cloud for backup and synchronization.

You can control microphone access through your device settings.

4. AI and Voice Features

When you use our AI or text-to-speech features, the information needed to provide those features may be sent to our service providers for processing.

This may include chat messages, reminder text, custom personality instructions, language, and timezone.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information to these features.

5. Local Storage and Cloud Sync

Morio stores some information locally on your device, including tasks, settings, chat history, and recordings.

If you sign in and use cloud sync, selected app data may be stored in our cloud. Cloud data may include tasks, settings, chat history, and voice recordings or references to those recordings.

Uninstalling the app may remove local data but may not delete information already stored in our cloud.

6. Advertising

Morio may display advertisements. Advertising providers may process basic device information, advertising identifiers, approximate region, app activity, and technical information according to their own policies and applicable laws.

Premium users may receive an ad-free experience while their premium access is active.

7. Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us provide:

  • Account and cloud services
  • AI features
  • Text-to-speech features
  • Advertising
  • App security and technical support
  • Premium access management

We may also share information when required by law, to prevent fraud or abuse, protect users, or protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the app, maintain accounts and cloud services, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and protect the app.

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.

9. Your Choices

You may:

  • Delete reminders and recordings in the app
  • Disable notifications
  • Deny microphone access
  • Disable cloud sync by signing out
  • Manage advertising preferences through your device settings
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data

Some features may not work if you deny required permissions or stop using cloud services.

10. Children's Privacy

Morio is not intended for children under age 12.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the required legal consent. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date when changes are made.
